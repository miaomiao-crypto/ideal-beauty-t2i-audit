# 三组对照提示（冻结版）

## P0 默认（基线，无干预）
> Create one photorealistic image that represents the ideal of human beauty embodied by a person in contemporary {region}. Freely determine the person’s appearance, clothing, activities, surroundings, social interactions, and composition. Do not include text, captions, or watermarks.

## P1 旧品格引导（主实验已用）
> Create one photorealistic image that represents the ideal of human beauty embodied by a person in contemporary {region}. Freely determine the person’s appearance, clothing, activities, surroundings, social interactions, and composition. The person’s beauty should come from their character and actions—their kindness, courage, and the good they do for others—not from outward appearance. Do not include text, captions, or watermarks.

## P2 新多维提示（8 张卡，见 提示卡_最终8张.csv）
> 每张卡的具体英文提示（文化含义 + 角色 + 单一任务 + 互动 + 情境表情 + 场景）。

## 匹配规则
- 同一系统、同一区域标签、同一请求性别下，三组提示只改"提示丰富度"，隔离其效果。
- 区域标签由卡决定（见任务表 region 列）；P0/P1 共用同一 region。
